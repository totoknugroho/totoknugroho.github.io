01 / THE NEXT STEP

After Japan, I knew that I wanted to continue research.

But I also knew that I did not want to simply repeat my master's work.

The questions had become broader.

How can we combine information from different physiological signals? How can brain and cardiovascular measurements complement each other? And how can we make physiological measurements more meaningful for real human problems?

These questions eventually brought me to Melbourne.

UNIVERSITY OF MELBOURNE

Biomedical
Engineering.

A new country, a new laboratory, and a new stage of the journey.

02 / QUESTIONS

My doctoral journey is expected to build upon the questions that emerged from my master's research.

I am particularly interested in multimodal physiological sensing, including the relationship between brain and cardiovascular signals.

EEG and ECG.

Brain activity and heart activity.

Signals that can be studied individually, but may become more informative when considered together.
