00 / PROLOGUE

I didn't always know where I was going.

My journey started with engineering physics and medical instrumentation in Indonesia.

Somewhere along the way, I became fascinated by the signals produced by the human body — especially the brain.

That curiosity took me from Indonesia to Japan, where I learned to become a researcher, and eventually to Australia, where a new chapter in biomedical engineering is about to begin.

This website is not a CV.

It is a record of how I got here.
