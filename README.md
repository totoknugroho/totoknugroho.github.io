## Where It Began

Growing up, I watched someone close to me live with epilepsy. Seeing how exhaustion and stress could shape everyday life taught me something no textbook could:

> Physiological signals are not just numbers on a screen. Behind every signal is a person, a story, and sometimes a struggle you can't see from the outside.

That understanding quietly shaped every question I'd go on to ask.

During my master's research in Japan, I studied **EEG-based chronic stress detection** — trying to understand how something as invisible as stress could be read through brain activity. It taught me that biomedical engineering isn't only about building technology. It's about asking the right questions *about people*.

Now, heading into a Ph.D. in Australia, I want to keep exploring brain signals, physiological sensing, and computational methods to better understand human health.

## Why I Created This Website

Academic papers show the destination.  
They rarely show the road that led there.

This is my attempt to document that road — the decisions, uncertainties, mistakes, lessons, research, applications, travels, and small moments that shaped me along the way.

I'm building it so that:
- 🪞 I can look back one day and remember not just *what* I achieved, but *why* I started.
- 🌏 Other students — from Indonesia and beyond — starting their own journey might see one path (undergrad → Japan → research → Ph.D. abroad) and feel a little less alone in theirs.
